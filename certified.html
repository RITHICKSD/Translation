What Makes a Translation "Certified"?

A certified translation includes a signed Affidavit of Accuracy on official company letterhead declaring that the translated text is an exact legal rendering of the original document.

  • Official ATA Member Seal & Stamp
  • Unique Verification Tracking Code
  • Optional Notarization & Apostille
